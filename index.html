Our story

Rail Trail Bake House is a neighborhood bakery for walkers, riders, runners and everyone in between. We bake from scratch with simple ingredients and a lot of patience.

Stop in for a loaf to take home, or a pastry to fuel the next few miles.

“Come hungry. Leave with a loaf.”
