Terms of sale

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version: order by Sunday at 6 pm, we bake Monday, and we bring it to your door Tuesday. Delivery is included in every price.

Ordering

Each week’s menu goes up on Wednesday, and orders close Sunday at 6 pm Eastern time for delivery that Tuesday. Your order is confirmed once payment goes through and you receive a confirmation email.

We bake in small batches. If we can’t fill part of your order, we’ll let you know and refund that part.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are in US dollars and include delivery within our delivery area.
  • There’s a $40 minimum per order.
  • Payment is taken in full when you check out, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, through Stripe.
  • Prices can change from week to week. You pay the price shown when you order.

Delivery

We deliver on Tuesdays between 10 am and 4 pm, within about a 15-minute drive of Hereford, Maryland. Checkout will tell you if your address is in the area. See how delivery works and our delivery and refunds policy for the details.

Allergens and ingredients

Our kitchen uses wheat, milk, butter, eggs and tree nuts, including pecans and almonds, and other common allergens. Everything we make is prepared in the same kitchen, so we can’t guarantee any item is free of an allergen. If you have a serious allergy, please email us before ordering.

Freshness and storage

Our bakes are made without preservatives and are best the day they arrive. Store bread in a paper bag at room temperature, and keep pies and anything with cream or fruit in the refrigerator.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page when you place an order applies to that order.
